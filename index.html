The problem

These failures stay silent — until they're dangerous.

Gas, drainage, and septic issues are usually noticed only after they turn serious. Without continuous monitoring, the first warning is often the damage itself.

Gas leaks

A small LPG or pipeline leak can build unnoticed and become a fire or health hazard — especially when no one is home to smell it.

Drain blockages

Blocked drains back up and overflow, creating unhygienic conditions and costly damage that could have been cleared early.

Septic overflow

A full septic tank causes odour, pollution, and health risks. Manual checking rarely catches it before it's already overflowing.

T + 0 min

A small anomaly begins

A faint gas trace, a slowing drain, or a rising tank level. Invisible to people nearby — and completely undetected without sensors.

T + hours

It quietly worsens

Concentration climbs, water backs up, the tank nears capacity. Still no smell, no visible sign, no alarm.

T + a day

First human notice

Someone smells gas, sees an overflow, or hits a backup. The problem is now urgent, messy, and expensive.

T + too late

It becomes a crisis

Safety danger, contamination, downtime, and repair bills — the outcomes NovaForge is built to prevent.

Why today's tools fall short

Alarms, inspections, and single-purpose gadgets leave gaps.

Traditional methods

  • Gas alarms only sound locally — useless if you're away from home.
  • Drains and tanks rely on manual inspection that happens too late.
  • Smart devices are often expensive and hard to install.
  • Each hazard needs a separate, single-purpose product.
  • No continuous history or early-warning trend data.

NovaForge

  • Alerts reach your phone anywhere, instantly — not just a local beep.
  • Continuous, automatic monitoring replaces manual checking.
  • One affordable system designed for easy setup.
  • Gas, drainage, and septic — unified in a single app.
  • Trends and history flag problems before they escalate.
